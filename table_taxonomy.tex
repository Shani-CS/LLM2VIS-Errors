  &
  & Misapplies\newline (34, 1)

\SetCell[r=2]{bg=taxData} Grouping or Binning Error\newline (6, 0)

  &
  & Misapplies\newline (5, 0)

%% ------------------------------------------------------------- filter (2)
\SetCell[r=2]{bg=taxData} Filter Error\newline (134, 26)
  & \SetCell[r=2]{} Using the wrong filter or misapplying a correct filter.

  & Ignores the filter in the prompt and/or applies the wrong filter.

  &
  & Misapplies\newline (129, 26)
  & Applies the required filter incorrectly.
  & Filters using the string ``final,'' which doesn't narrow to the final round, but also includes quarter-final and semi-final [A-S45] \\

\SetCell[r=2]{bg=taxChart} Sorting Error\newline (9, 14)

  & Does not apply any sorting or reference it in the code provided [A-S28] \\
  &
  & Misapplies\newline (8, 11)
  & Applies the required sorting incorrectly.
  & Sorts by sub-category in alphabetical order instead of ascending order by count [B-110-0] \\
%% ----------------------------------------------------------- encoding (2)
\SetCell[r=2]{bg=taxChart} Encoding Error\newline (77, 8)

  &
  & Misapplies\newline (38, 6)

\SetCell[r=2]{bg=taxDesc} Interpretation Error\newline (13, 20)
  & \SetCell[r=2]{} In some responses, an additional paragraph of text follows the descriptive sentence.

  & Makes a statement that goes beyond the available data without evidence.
  & Model response: ``The plot reveals \textbf{how different countries might prioritize the number of cylinders or focus on designs with certain acceleration levels.''}  [B-16-1] \\
  &
  & Fabrication\newline (9, 17)
  & Hallucinates - provably wrong given the provided information.
  & Model response: ``Vehicles with 4 and 5 cylinders tend to have higher average MPG, with \textbf{5-cylinder vehicles achieving the highest average}.'' [B-4-1] \\
